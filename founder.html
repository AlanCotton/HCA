Jane Heath · Co-founder
The founder

She didn't just build a business.
She helped create a sector.

Jane Heath is a successful entrepreneur. She co-founded HCA in 1999 - one of the UK's first agency new business agencies - thereby creating a sector, not just a business.

A business mentor and go-to-market strategist, Jane has a proven track record of driving revenue, profit and performance.

Having seen agency new business change many, many times, Jane is clear about what AI does well, what it doesn't and what needs a person. Today she helps agencies use AI where it makes a difference, while keeping the strategic thought and high-level conversations that win business firmly in the hands of humans.
